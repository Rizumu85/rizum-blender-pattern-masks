Layers and edges

Every layer draws along its own edges. Try each idea below on the skirt piece.

The layer list

Each pattern is a row in the list.

  1. 1NameAlso used as the file name.
  2. 2ColorThe preview color.
  3. 3Edges toggleShows the layer's marked edges.
  4. 4EyeShows the layer. A hidden layer isn't baked.
  5. 5+Adds Stitches (one per object), Line Art or Grid.
  6. 6−Removes the selected layer.
  7. 7Marks switchIn the panel header: hides or shows every layer's marked edges and corners at once. Each layer keeps its own toggle.
  8. 8PlayIn the panel header: turns live updates on and off.
  9. 9RefreshShows while live updates are off: rebuilds once.
  10. 10⌄Clear All Layers: removes every object's layers, marked edges and previews from the scene.

Edges row

Select edges in Edit Mode, pick the layer in the list, then use the Edges row.

Each layer keeps its own edges.

  1. 1+Adds the selected edges to the layer.
  2. 2−Takes them out of the layer.
  3. 3ArrowSelects all of the layer's edges.
  4. 4×Clears the layer's edges.
  5. 5LinkFollows another layer's edges: see Reference. It shows pressed while the layer follows one.

Reference

A layer can use the edges of another one, and follows when those change.

To leave a few of them out, select them and press −: they turn red here, and the other layer keeps them.

In the link menu the layer being followed is ticked; Clear Reference stops following it.

Sharp corners

Where two edges meet at a tip, a line rounds the corner. To keep it sharp, mark the vertex on the Sharp Corners row.

The marks are shared by all layers.

  1. 1+Marks the selected tips.
  2. 2−Clears them.
  3. 3ArrowSelects every marked tip.
  4. 4AutoMarks every likely tip.
  5. 5Show CornersShows the marked corners in the view.

Flip Side

Flip Side moves every line to the other side of its edges.

Use it for edges inside the mesh. A border has no cloth on its other side, so its lines disappear.

Shared by all layers.
