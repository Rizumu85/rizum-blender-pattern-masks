Notes and limits

Good to know before you bake.

Mesh

  • Modifiers are ignored. Apply them on a copy first.
  • Lines skip notches narrower than twice their distance from the edge.
  • Where cloth folds over itself, stitches bake only on the layer they belong to.

Grid

  • Follow Loops forks where the piece widens. Larger squares mean fewer forks.
  • Unrolled takes a second or two to compute, in the background.

Bake

  • An 8192 bake needs about 2 GB of free memory.
  • A face that crosses a UDIM tile border is baked into one tile only.
