Bake and use

Files

File names are made of the object, material and layer names.

  • With several objects selected, one bake does them all, each with its own layers and settings.
  • Once the bake settings are right, fold them away to keep the panel tidy.

Baking runs in the background, so you can keep working.

Using the masks

  • Substance 3D Painter: import the PNG and use it as a mask on a fill layer. Re-import it after each new bake.
  • Clip Studio Paint: set Background to White, bake, open the PNG and use Edit › Convert brightness to opacity: the white turns clear and the marks stay. (It can't open a transparent PNG that holds very little.)
  • Photoshop and other 2D apps: Transparent puts the mask in the alpha channel.
